Platinum roadmap — six stages to Vice Got a Price!

One playthrough, no mission replay, cheats off. Keep manual saves at chapter boundaries. Stages are ordered so the single potentially-missable moment is handled in Stage 1, and the 100% grind lands last.

Missable watch

⚠ One potentially-missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude

In Chapter 1, the morning after Down to Earth, Lucia mentions meditating in the kitchen at Jason's house and walks to the balcony. Follow her and choose Accept. That moment disappears after Chapter 1. PowerPyx and GTABase both flag it as the only possibly-missable trophy moment; whether the trophy strictly requires it is still unconfirmed — do it anyway, then save.

Everything else can be earned after the story: free roam continues, there are no online trophies, and nothing on the 100% checklist is reported missable. Actions Speak Louder is not missable — it is a 500-action Criminal Profile counter that keeps rising in free roam; check it last.

Save discipline: 15 manual slots + 1 autosave, no mission replay. Keep a save at the start and end of every chapter, and never use cheats on a trophy run — they disable trophies and saving.
