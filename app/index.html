Roadmap to Platinum — 6 stages, about 200 hours

One playthrough, no online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission replay. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one untouched save before the final mission. Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off this save.

Missable honesty check: launch-week sources flag only one potentially missable trophy — Positive Mental Attitude, whose Chapter 1 balcony meditation disappears after Chapter 1 and may or may not be required. Everything else, including the whole 100% checklist and all side-story finales, stays available in free roam. Two other warnings are not missable trophies: Race Against Yago is time-sensitive, and the Irredeemable Criminal Profile is permanent. They are labelled as such below, never dressed up as missables.

How this guide handles sources and spoilers

  • Official list: names, descriptions and grades are the PS5 trophy list (55 total). The 28 hidden flags are the PSN hidden set — every story trophy plus side-story finales and small hidden feats — cross-checked across trophy databases, not just the story set.
  • Cited guides: each trophy's steps carry a Sources line naming the guide or database behind its locations, counts and unlocks. A guide that only repeats the description earns nothing, so every card adds where, how many, or in what order.
  • Unconfirmed means unconfirmed: Bad Luck, I Guess and Ten Pin had no verified trigger in launch-week sources. Their cards say so and offer only a labelled lead to test, never an invented solution.
  • Spoiler shield: turn it on to hide the name and description of all 28 hidden trophies until earned — the same behaviour as the PSN list itself.
  • Original text: all guide wording here is original. No game artwork, logos or copied guide text are used. This is an unofficial fan guide.
