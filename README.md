An offline guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI, built for the [make.fun gta6-trophy-guide-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-trophy-guide-01).

## What it does

- **Every trophy, officially**: all 55 trophies (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze) with their official PlayStation names, descriptions and grades, as listed via Exophase and PSNProfiles.
- **A cited guide for each trophy**, written for this entry: what to do, in what order, and when it can be missed — with its sources named beside the text.
- **Roadmap to the platinum**: six stages in the recommended earning order, from the story through the missable stranger strands to 100% completion.
- **Missable flags**: character strands and the VCIA departure are flagged from the official descriptions, with the advice to finish them before the final mission.
- **Tracker**: tick trophies off, progress saved in your browser (localStorage), exportable to a JSON file and importable back.
- **Filters and spoiler shield**: filter by kind (story, missable, collectibles, activities, online), search, sort, and a spoiler setting that hides story trophies until they are earned.

The app is plain static files under `app/` with no build step and no network requests: serve `app/` with any static HTTP server (for example `node serve.ts` from this repository) and open `index.html`.

Fan-made guide; not affiliated with Rockstar Games or PlayStation. No Rockstar logos or artwork are used.
